3. What the numbers mean

Symmetric AES-GCM is measured in megabytes per second, not operations: it encrypts bulk data quickly, and most modern CPUs accelerate it in hardware (AES-NI). This is why AES protects files, disks and network traffic.

Asymmetric RSA and ECC operations are measured per operation, not per megabyte. They are used only for small values: exchanging keys and signing. RSA's public-key operations (encrypt, verify) are fast, but its private-key operations and key generation are slow. ECC reaches RSA-3072 security with a 256-bit key and generates keys thousands of times faster.

DES and 3DES are not available in Web Crypto because browsers no longer support obsolete ciphers, which matches the research's conclusion that they should not be used in new systems.
